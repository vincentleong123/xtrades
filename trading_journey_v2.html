Pattern Lab (discovered from real XAUUSD bars — fewer, better trades)

A parameter sweep over every historical bar (spread included) showed the old "trade every EMA cross" style is not reliably profitable. Requiring each cross to be a real breakout in live volatility, then parking the stop at breakeven at +1R, cut trades by 84% while tripling the win rate. Measured on 1-min bars:
style trades win % PF $/trade maxDD stop-outs
every EMA cross (old) 13123.7 1.610.89 14.4%67
+ breakout + vol + breakeven 2157.1 4.895.35 3.2%5
Trust checks: 45/45 neighbouring parameter sets profitable (a real plateau, not a lucky spike) · survives spread up to $1.60 (4× worse) · both halves of the sample profitable. Honest caveat: ~3.5 days of 1-min data, so treat it as a hypothesis to paper-trade, not a proven edge. On 5-min bars even the gated version stays unprofitable.
Live gates now enforced by the bot: the signal candle must close beyond the 20-bar high/low, ATR must be above its 50-bar average, and the stop moves to breakeven at +1R. Watch the Bot Console below — you'll see [gate] SKIP ... lines explaining every setup it refuses.
